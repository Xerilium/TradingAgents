## Objective
Use multilingual Laya for final research review. Summarize evidence and enforce the 8192-token maximum per complete question sequence, including questions, criteria, metadata and special tokens. Never silently truncate.

## Findings
Installed English Laya defaults to 512 tokens per question. The observed 1024 input tokens totals two sequences. The adapter calls router.predict without a length override. Multilingual defaults to 1024 but supports 8192. Gateway decide exposes model selection but no length or token-preflight setting. The latest META packet has roughly 97000 characters; seven saved reviews requested human review.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Force multilingual for this review and verify effective capacity of 8192 without changing unrelated routing or confidence thresholds.
2. Summarize the exact rating, supporting evidence, counterarguments, contradictions, source references, unresolved gaps, quote/date/feed context and execution conditions. Distinguish research recommendation from permission to trade.
3. Count with the actual multilingual tokenizer and Laya sequence builder. Include all encoded overhead and reserve headroom. Detect truncation of questions/options as well as state.
4. Fail before inference when oversized or unverifiable. Bounded resummarization only; no silent clipping or uncontrolled retries. Respect cancellation and native timeouts; never hammer the backend.
5. Persist exact summary, source fingerprint, routing and token-budget evidence with the result; retain all original reports and expose review evidence in the UI.
6. Record failing regression tests against prior behavior before completing implementation. Cover exact limits, multibyte text, question/metadata overhead, malformed summaries, invalid/missing preflight, overflow exhaustion, cancellation, timeouts, routing and persistence. Require passing targeted and integration checks and independent verification.
7. Compare summary to a saved packet. Bounded live verification only after offline checks, without interrupting active research. Preserve paper-only execution, manual submission and broker risk guards. No automatic research batches or orders.
